# The Annual Fees run creates one Invoice per address and carries on past failures

The Annual Fees run bills all active Persons in one go. The decisions behind it:

- **One Invoice per address.** Persons sharing a complete address are grouped on one Invoice with one Invoice Line each, including Persons who pay 0; a Person with an incomplete address gets their own Invoice (and a warning). The recipient is the Payer (when a Person on the address has dependents), otherwise the Family payer (a Main Payer first, then the longest-standing Family member), otherwise the longest-standing member. An Invoice whose total is 0 is not created.
- **A Payer receives the Invoice of the Persons they pay for.** A Person with a Payer is left out of address grouping; their Invoice Line goes on the Payer's Invoice, even when the Payer lives elsewhere or is not an active member (then the Payer is recipient only and gets no Invoice Line of their own). The Payer is the recipient of that Invoice.
- **A Family pays once.** Only the longest-standing Family member of an address is billed the Family fee; the other Family members of that address get an Invoice Line of 0, so every member stays visible on the Invoice. Lines of 0 are never dropped (Honorary, 100 % Discount); a Discount on a Person who pays 0 is ignored with a warning.
- **One transaction per Invoice, failures do not stop the run.** Each Invoice is created in its own transaction through the normal creation path (ADR 0004 still applies). A failed one is rolled back and listed in the summary; the others are kept. A rerun is therefore not atomic and can be repeated for what is missing.
- **No duplicate check.** The run does not look for an earlier Invoice of the same year; the treasurer confirms before starting. Invoices are immutable and have no year, so there is nothing reliable to compare against.
- **Fees are constants.** The amounts per Membership live in the code (_MembershipFeeRules_), not in settings or the database; changing a fee is a code change. Only Discounts are stored data.
