# An Invoice Line carries the account of its later Entry as a hint

An Invoice bills a Person, but nothing tells the treasurer where the money belongs once it arrives: Entries are only created on Transactions, and a Transaction is not linked to the invoice it settles. Every Invoice Line therefore carries a required Account from the Chart of Accounts — the account its amount will be booked to when the matching payment comes in.

The Account is a booking hint, not a booking. Creating an Invoice writes no Entry; when the payment arrives, the treasurer books the Transaction and uses the invoice's positions to decide the Entries (one per position, with that position's Account and amount). The CAMT import is later intended to create those Entries automatically once an imported Transaction matches an open Invoice; the stored Account is the data that matching will consume.

We chose the whole Chart of Accounts over income accounts only: a Transaction's Entries can be a combination of Income and Expense, so a position's account must not be constrained by the Invoice's direction. Only active Accounts are selectable, and the server re-checks every position's account against the Chart of Accounts file (exists and active), so an Invoice can never reference a deleted or retired account.

The column is not nullable: positions that predate this rule are backfilled with 3400 (Jahresbeiträge), an approximation chosen by the club rather than inventing per-invoice history. The account is internal — it is shown in the app's detail view but not on the rendered PDF — and a position's Account does not count as a "use" for the Chart of Accounts-vs-Entries startup check (ADR 0001, ADR 0002), which stays scoped to Entries: a hint feeds no calculation, and a position pointing at a later-deleted account is reported when the future auto-booking tries to use it.
